Aussie Online Games is a mobile game developed and operated by JLPV STUDIO (the “developer”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Aussie Online Games (the “game”), including information processed through Firebase Analytics and OneSignal.

We do not offer account registration or display third-party advertising in the game. This does not mean that no data is collected: analytics and notification services may process device and usage information.

011. Information collected

Depending on your device, permissions, and the configuration of the services integrated into the game, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and other technical characteristics.
  • Identifiers: app-instance or installation identifiers, and push notification subscription identifiers or tokens.
  • Usage and gameplay information: app opens, sessions, screen interactions, and gameplay events recorded by analytics.
  • Approximate location: broad geographic information that may be inferred from network or device signals by service providers.
  • Notification information: subscription preferences, delivery status, and notification interactions.
  • Technical diagnostics: limited technical or performance information provided by integrated services.

Game scores, virtual coins, and rewards may also be processed locally or as part of gameplay events, depending on how a feature is implemented. The game does not require you to create an account.

022. Purposes of processing

We use the information described above to operate the game, understand general usage and engagement, improve gameplay and reliability, deliver notifications where enabled, prevent misuse, and respond to privacy requests or legal obligations.

033. Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to measure how the game is used. Firebase Analytics may process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, usage events, session data, and approximate geographic information. We use aggregated insights to evaluate engagement and improve the game. The exact data depends on Firebase settings and the events implemented in the game.

For details, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

044. OneSignal and push notifications

We use OneSignal to manage push notifications about game-related news, updates, or events. OneSignal may process push tokens, subscription identifiers, IP addresses, device and app information, language, time zone, notification subscription status, and delivery or interaction events. Notification delivery on Android may rely on Google’s messaging infrastructure.

Where Android requires notification permission, you can choose whether to grant it. You can also disable notifications later in your device settings. Disabling notifications does not necessarily delete information already held by a service provider.

For details, see the OneSignal Privacy Policy.

055. Advertising and accounts

The game does not display third-party advertisements and does not require registration or a user account. We do not use the game to request your name, email address, or password for account creation. If you contact our support team by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond.

066. Sharing and service providers

Information is transmitted to Google (Firebase Analytics) and OneSignal as necessary for their respective services. These providers may process information on our behalf and under their own applicable terms. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the rights and security of users and the game. We do not state that information is never shared, because data is transmitted to these service providers.

077. Data retention and deletion

Data retention varies by data type and the settings of Firebase Analytics and OneSignal. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here or as required by applicable law. You may request information about, or deletion of, personal data associated with your use of the game by contacting support@jlpvstudio.cloud. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, including where identifying an installation without an account requires additional information from you. Some data may remain temporarily in provider backups or where retention is legally required.

088. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information, including the security measures available through our service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

099. International processing

Google and OneSignal may process information in countries outside your place of residence. Where applicable, we take steps to ensure international transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards required by law.

1010. Children’s privacy

The game’s intended audience and content rating are identified in its Google Play listing. We recognise that children’s data requires special protection and do not knowingly process personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child’s personal information has been processed inappropriately, contact us at the address below. We will investigate and take appropriate action.

1111. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. You can control push notifications in your device settings. To exercise privacy rights, email support@jlpvstudio.cloud.

1212. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date.
